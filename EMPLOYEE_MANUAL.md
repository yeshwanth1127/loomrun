# Loomrun — Employee Manual

*A plain-English guide for team members using Loomrun day to day.*

---

## 1. What is Loomrun?

Loomrun is the app your company uses to run the whole journey of a customer order, from first enquiry to delivered product: someone gets in touch → that's a **Lead** → your team follows up and (once things move further along) a price gets quoted → the order gets built and shipped.

**Your part in that journey is the front end of it: finding out what a customer wants and getting them on the phone.** Your access in Loomrun is deliberately focused on four things — Leads, Follow-ups, Telecaller, and the Loomrun AI assistant. Everything past that (quotes, invoices, production, money) is handled by your company's Owner. This manual covers exactly what you can do.

---

## 2. Logging In & Finding Your Way Around

- Log in with the email and password your Owner/manager gave you.
- Your left-hand menu shows exactly four items: **Leads, Follow-ups, Telecaller,** and **Loomrun AI**. That's the full extent of your access, regardless of the specific role your Owner gave you (Sales, Telecaller, Production, or Viewer) — they all see the same four pages today. This isn't a display quirk: if you try to type in a different page's web address directly, the app will send you back to Leads.
- At the top of most pages is a **date filter** — switch between "All" (everything ever recorded) and a specific day, which narrows down what's shown.
- The sun/moon icon toggles light or dark mode. The sign-out icon logs you out.
- If you belong to more than one company, a dropdown at the bottom of the menu lets you switch between them.

## 3. Managing Leads (your sales pipeline)

The **Leads** page is where every enquiry lives, from first contact to won or lost.

### Ways to look at it
- **Board view** — a Kanban-style board with a column per stage; drag a lead's card into a new column to move it forward.
- **Table view** — a sortable list, better for scanning lots of leads at once.

### What you can do
- **Add a new lead**: name, source, phone, email, city, what they're interested in, estimated quantity, notes, and estimated deal value.
- **Search and filter**: by name/phone, by where the lead came from, by pipeline stage, or by lead score range.
- **Open a lead** to see a full detail panel with:
  - A **quick call button** (dials straight from your phone/browser)
  - A **WhatsApp button** (opens a chat with them on your own WhatsApp)
  - Editable fields — click any field (phone, email, city, product interest, follow-up date, notes, etc.) to update it right there
  - A **"Log Activity"** box to record a note, call, WhatsApp, or email you just had with them
  - A full **history/timeline** of everything that's happened on this lead

### Understanding lead stages
Leads move through: **New → Contacted → Requirement Collected → Quoted → Negotiation → Sample Sent → Won** (or **Lost** at any point).

### Lead score
Every lead has a 0–100 score (shown as a colored ring — green is hot, amber is warm, red is cold) that tells you how promising it looks, based on where it came from and how complete its details are. Use it to decide who to call first.

## 4. Follow-Ups

The **Follow-ups** page is a simple worklist of every lead where someone promised a callback. It's automatically sorted into:
- **Overdue** (should've called by now)
- **Today**
- **Upcoming**
- **No date set**

Each row shows quick **Call** and **WhatsApp** buttons so you can act immediately. This page is just for viewing — there's nothing to configure here, it's meant to make sure nobody gets forgotten.

## 5. Telecaller — Making & Logging Calls

The **Telecaller** page is your workspace for placing calls and recording what happened. This is also where you can hand a customer their catalog or quotation over WhatsApp without needing access to the WhatsApp or Quotations pages.

### Placing a call
1. Search for and pick the lead you want to call (their details auto-fill).
2. Choose how to call, depending on what your company has set up:
   - **In-browser call** — call directly from your computer, with mute and hang-up buttons and a timer.
   - **Click-to-call** — your own phone rings first, then connects you to the lead.
   - **Manual / Own phone** — just dial from your own mobile and log the result here afterward.
   - **AI Auto-Call** — lets an AI voice assistant place the call for you.

### After the call
Fill in the **Save Call** form:
- The call outcome: **Connected, No Answer, Busy, Wrong Number, Not Interested, Callback Scheduled,** or **Qualified**
- How long the call lasted, and any notes
- Update the lead's stage, contact details, or estimated value while it's fresh
- If the call **Connected**, you can tick a box to instantly WhatsApp the customer your **product catalog** and/or their **latest quotation** — this works automatically even though you don't have your own access to the Quotations or WhatsApp pages.

### Tracking your day
A summary panel shows the day's total calls and a breakdown by outcome. If your role is Telecaller, this shows **your own** calls; other roles see the whole team's calls. Click any past call in the log to see full details, including the recording and AI transcript if it was an AI-placed call.

## 6. Loomrun AI Assistant

The **Loomrun AI** page is a chat assistant you can talk to in plain language:

- Ask it questions like *"show me hot leads in Mumbai"* or *"what's the status of this lead?"*
- On higher plans, you can also ask it to actually do things with leads — *"move Rajesh's lead to Negotiation"*, *"create a lead for this new enquiry"*, *"schedule a follow-up for tomorrow"*. It will always show you exactly what it's about to do and wait for you to click **Confirm** before anything actually happens — nothing changes without your say-so.
- You can start new conversations, search past ones, and delete old ones.
- There's a microphone button if you'd rather talk than type, and it can read replies back out loud.
- **What it won't do for you:** the AI follows the same rules as the rest of the app. If you ask it to create or send a quotation/invoice, or look up the product catalog, it doesn't have access to those actions on your account — those only work when your Owner is the one asking.

How much you can ask it per day, and whether it can take actions at all, depends on your company's subscription plan — if it tells you a limit's been reached or a feature needs an upgrade, that's a plan setting your Owner controls.

## 7. Getting Help

If something looks broken or a button won't do what you expect, check for a small explanation text near it first (many buttons explain why they're disabled). If you're still stuck, let your Owner or manager know — they may need to reach out to Loomrun's support team on your behalf.
