Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 May 2026  |  Last updated: 29 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Loomrun (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our web application and related services at https://loomrun.exora.solutions (the “Service”).

Loomrun is operated by Exora Solutions. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@exora.solutions.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to:

  • Users who register for or sign in to a Loomrun workspace;
  • Organization administrators and team members invited to a workspace;
  • Individuals whose information is processed through integrations you connect (for example, sales leads from advertising platforms or messaging channels).

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), and role within an organization.
  • Organization and business data: company name, branding assets, product catalog, quotations, production records, and team membership details you enter in the Service.
  • Lead and customer data: contact details and form responses imported from connected sources (such as Meta Lead Ads, WhatsApp, or manual entry), including phone numbers, email addresses, and campaign metadata.
  • Integration credentials: OAuth tokens and configuration data needed to connect third-party services (for example, Meta/Facebook Page tokens for lead retrieval). We store these securely and use them only to provide the integration features you enable.
  • Usage and technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, log data, and actions taken in the application for security, troubleshooting, and service improvement.

3. How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service;
  • Authenticate users and enforce access controls within organizations;
  • Import, display, and manage leads and related workflows you configure;
  • Generate documents (such as quotations) and send communications you initiate through connected channels;
  • Respond to support requests and protect against fraud, abuse, or security incidents;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-party services and Meta integrations

When you connect integrations, data is shared with those providers according to their policies and your configuration:

  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram): If you connect Meta Lead Ads, we receive lead data and Page-related tokens as permitted by the permissions you grant during Facebook Login. Meta’s use of data is governed by Meta’s Privacy Policy.
  • WhatsApp and telephony providers: If enabled, message or call-related data is processed through the providers you configure.
  • Infrastructure: The Service runs on servers we control; database and file storage are used to persist your workspace data.

You are responsible for ensuring you have a lawful basis to collect and process lead and customer data imported into Loomrun, including providing any notices required to data subjects.

5. Legal bases (where applicable)

If you are in a jurisdiction that requires a legal basis for processing (such as the EEA or UK), we rely on: performance of our contract with you (providing the Service), your instructions when connecting integrations, legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, and compliance with legal obligations.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as your organization uses the Service or as needed to provide features you use. You may request deletion of your account or workspace data by contacting us. Some data may be retained where required by law or for legitimate business purposes (for example, security logs).

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including access controls, encrypted transport (HTTPS), and hashed passwords. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email support@exora.solutions. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. International transfers

Your information may be processed on servers located in India or other countries where we or our service providers operate. By using the Service, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to jurisdictions that may have different data protection laws.

10. Children

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
