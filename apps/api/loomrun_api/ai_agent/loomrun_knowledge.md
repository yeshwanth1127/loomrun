# Loomrun product knowledge

Loomrun is a multi-tenant manufacturing CRM and operations platform for Indian manufacturers and exporters.

## Core workflow

1. **Leads** — Capture from Meta Ads, website forms, referrals, IndiaMart, and manual entry. Stages move leads through the sales funnel with scoring, tags, notes, and follow-up scheduling.
2. **Quotations** — Build line-item quotations, generate branded PDFs, send to customers.
3. **Invoices** — Convert accepted quotations to invoices with branded templates.
4. **Production** — Track orders through a 13-stage production pipeline from pending through shipping.
5. **Payments & expenses** — Record payments against orders and track expenses for per-order P&L.
6. **CEO dashboard** — Real-time KPIs across pipeline, production, and finance.

## Team roles

- **OWNER** — Full access including team, brand assets, telephony, connectors, subscription.
- **SALES** — Leads, quotations, WhatsApp.
- **TELECALLER** — Leads, follow-ups, telecaller workspace.
- **PRODUCTION** — Production pipeline.
- **VIEWER** — Read-oriented access.

## Messaging & calling

- WhatsApp messaging with templates for quotations, invoices, and follow-ups.
- Telecaller workflow with call logging.
- Platform-managed telephony (Twilio, VAPI AI voice, Exotel) where configured.

## Brand & documents

- Brand assets: logo, legal name, address, bank details, UPI QR.
- Document templates for quotations and invoices.

## Subscription plans

- **Free** — Default trial; AI chat disabled; up to 10 users.
- **Growth** (₹2,899/month) — Full CRM/ops suite; minimal AI chat for basic questions; 10 users included (₹750/extra user/month).
- **Scale** (₹5,799/month) — Everything in Growth plus advanced multilingual AI, voice agents, Google/Meta integrations, event automations, priority support.

Plans are assigned by Loomrun platform admins. Owners request upgrades from the Subscription page.
